# Typed Discrete Guide Identities

Discrete source keys now retain typed raw scientific fields/categories, observed
pairs, sign keys, endpoint labels, Regression observation/fit keys and supplemental
layer labels. Raw field/category text and exact supplied layer bytes use immutable
shared values with checked storage layouts and fallible source copies. Display
aliases never determine identity or categorical membership.

## Combined Title And Literal Authority

Combined merging first establishes the compatible primary raw title identity and
resolved title across all contributors. Supplemental-only contributors do not
choose that title. Their already resolved labels attach after primary entries,
under the actual primary title, or literal `series` when no primary exists. This
is independent of contributor order. Exact duplicate identities require identical
display text and complete visible glyphs; distinct identities with equal display
labels reject, including a layer colliding with a categorical entry.

PointLayers retain their source Plot's existing categorical-versus-literal label
resolution. Endpoint and Regression strings remain literal. Bar sign, endpoint,
Regression and layer identities cannot accidentally become categorical hue
members. The old arbitrary Single/Pair/Literal identity carrier and guessed
`contributes_hue_level` flag are removed.

This leaf changes identity and Combined title authority. The existing flat tuple,
per-entry title storage, result field and measurement transport remain the next
atomic retirement; this evidence does not claim section descriptors, Separate
projection, final-descriptor font census or public composition selection.
